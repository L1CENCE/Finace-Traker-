# Personal Finance Tracker V2 💰

A simple, interactive command-line Python application designed to help you take control of your finances by tracking your current month's budget, evaluating savings goals, and planning ahead for next month.

## ✨ Features

* **Current Month Tracking:** Input your monthly post-tax income and total expenses to automatically calculate your remaining balance.
* **Savings Goal Evaluation:** Set a monthly savings goal and instantly find out if you've met it or how much more you need to save[cite: 1].
* **Investment Suggestions:** Get quick guidance on investment options like stocks, bonds, or mutual funds if you choose to invest your remaining balance[cite: 1].
* **Next Month Budget Forecasting:** Plan ahead by projecting expected income and expenses for the upcoming month[cite: 1].
* **Financial Warnings:** Receive alerts if your projected expenses exceed your earnings to help you adjust your budget proactively[cite: 1].
